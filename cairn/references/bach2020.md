# bach2020 — Bach et al. (2020): the 36-item PID5BF+M, its anankastia items and domain rule

**Provenance.** Ingested 2026-10-03 by M157 from
`cairn/references/sources/bach2020a.pdf` (gitignored), the typeset article from the
publisher. Pagination: journal pages 179 to 188, printed in the running heads. Every
anchor below is the printed journal page. PDF page 3 is p. 181, and PDF page 5 is
p. 183.
Extraction: verified 2026-10-03 against the source, from `pdftotext -layout` output of pp. 181 and 183 — observed 2026-10-03.

**Citation.** Bach, B., Kerber, A., Aluja, A., Bastiaens, T., Keeley, J. W., Claes,
L., Fossati, A., Gutierrez, F., Oliveira, S. E. S., Pires, R., Riegel, K. D.,
Rolland, J.-P., Roskam, I., Sellbom, M., Somma, A., Spanemberg, L., Strus, W.,
Thimm, J. C., Wright, A. G. C., & Zimmermann, J. (2020). International assessment
of DSM-5 and ICD-11 personality disorder traits: Toward a common nosology in
DSM-5.1. *Psychopathology, 53*(3-4), 179-188. https://doi.org/10.1159/000507589

**Role.** The development paper for the PID5BF+M, which `score_pid5()` cites. It
confirms the six anankastia items and the domain rule of the key sheet
([fuberlin2020pid5bfpm](fuberlin2020pid5bfpm.md)). It also gives the English facet
names. It does not print the full 36-item key, which is in its online Appendix A.

## Extracted values

- Item count and structure, p. 181: the form "comprises 36 items, which delineate
  18 facets (2 items per facet)".
- Domain rule, p. 181: "The 6 domain scores are estimated using average scores for
  each domain's 3 primary facets."
- Anankastia items, p. 181, as PID-5 numbers: perfectionism uses "item 123 and item
  176", rigidity uses "item 140 and item 220", and orderliness uses "item 34 and
  item 115".
- The other 30 items, p. 181: they are the items "from the original PID5BF+
  representing the 5 DSM-5 trait domains", which "remained unchanged".
- Full key, p. 181: "The modified PID5BF+ scoring key is included in online
  supplementary Appendix A."
- Facet names, Table 2, p. 183, in row order: Emotional lability, Anxiousness,
  Separation insecurity, Withdrawal, Anhedonia, Intimacy avoidance,
  Manipulativeness, Deceitfulness, Grandiosity, Irresponsibility, Impulsivity,
  Distractibility, Perfectionism, Rigidity, Orderliness, Unusual beliefs,
  Eccentricity, Perceptual dysregulation. Domain columns: Negative affectivity,
  Detachment, Antagonism, Disinhibition, Anankastia, Psychoticism.
