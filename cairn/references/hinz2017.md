**Role.** The source of the PHQ-15 scoring rule that `score_hitopdat()` applies, and of
the PHQ-15 cutoffs the authors chose (Table 5).

## Extracted values

- p. 28, "Instruments": each symptom is rated 0, 1 or 2, so "the total PHQ-15 score
  ranges from 0 to 30". "The item on menstruation was excluded from the analysis."
- p. 28, Table 2 note: "The range of PHQ-15 scores in this study range from 0 to 28 due
  to excluding the menstruation symptom." The battery also leaves that item out
  (SOURCES.md DAT-6), so its 14 items sum to 0 to 28.
- p. 28, "Sample characteristics": "If only one PHQ-15 item was missing, it was
  replaced with the rounded mean of the other items."
- p. 28, Table 2: total M 4.62, SD 3.59 for men, 6.31, 4.06 for women.

## Traces to

- `R/score_hitopdat.R`: the PHQ-15 raw score is the sum of its 14 items.
- `tests/testthat/test-score_hitopdat.R`: the helper's sum rule for the PHQ-15.
- `?score_hitopdat`: the departure from the paper's one-item fill, below.

## Open questions

- The paper fills one missing item with the other items' rounded mean and drops a form
  with two or more missing. `score_hitopdat()` prorates by the authors' rule instead
  (SOURCES.md "The authors' joint answers"), with no rounding. With one of 14 items
  missing the two differ only by the rounding.
