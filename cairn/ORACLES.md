| O-003 | simulation-coverage | `tests/testthat/test-interval_hitopsr.R:351` — "the interval covers the true score at its nominal rate over the reference population" | [schmukle2026](references/schmukle2026.md), simulation method p. 822 and Table 1 p. 822 | The primary oracle for an interval method. Generates under the model the source page records — `t ~ Normal(M, SD)`, `x = M + sqrt(rel) * (t - M) + Normal(0, SD * sqrt(1 - rel))` — swept over the lowest, median and highest reliability the shipped table carries (0.61, 0.84, 0.96) and two nominal levels (0.95, 0.80). Seed 20260828 + cell index, 200,000 replications per cell, fixed in the test; tolerance is the Monte-Carlo bound at four standard errors of a proportion (0.002 at 0.95, 0.0036 at 0.80). Marginal coverage only, which is what the method promises; the companion test at `:394` shows coverage at a fixed true score swinging 0.82 to 0.99 under the same model, so the distinction is pinned rather than asserted in prose. Discrimination checked 2026-08-28 by running the same sweep against four wrong estimators — Eq (5)'s shrink-by-`rel`, Eq (6)'s `SEE` half-width, Eq (3)'s no-shrink, and a half-width missing its square root — which returned 0.9413, 0.8744, 0.9362 and 0.7784 at reliability 0.61, all outside the tolerance. At 0.96 three of the four sit inside it, so the low-reliability cell is what gives this oracle its power and the sweep must keep it. |
