# M108: A module descriptor names its export's item columns

**Status:** done (2026-09-22, PR #119 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop/pull/119)

**Goal:** A researcher scores data from a Qualtrics or REDCap HiTOP-SR export by passing the descriptor the generator wrote, with no item column names typed out.

**Outcome:** The descriptor gains an optional `columns` field under format `"1.0"`. `write_descriptor_sidecar()` sets `columns` every time, as it does `item_order`. The REDCap generator writes its dictionary field names through `redcap_item_names()`, and the Qualtrics generator writes its `[[ID:]]` values through `qualtrics_item_ids()`. The Word generator writes no field, even from a module that carries one. `read_module_columns()` reads the field from the `simplifyVector = FALSE` parse onto the `columns` attribute and refuses 11 bad shapes under `hitop_module_file_bad_columns`. `write_module()` writes the attribute after `itemOrder` and refuses 5 bad shapes before it opens the path. If `items` is missing or `NULL`, `module_column_items()` gives `score_hitopsr()` and `reliability_hitopsr()` the module's columns. It aborts and says to pass `items` in four cases: no module, no attribute, `layout = "printed"`, or a data frame that lacks the module's names. Source notes: `vanderbilt2015redcapdd.md` and a first-hand Qualtrics export, `qualtrics2026exportheader.md`. Help pages, the modules article and NEWS are updated.

**Decisions:** D-067. At the amendment gate, AC1 took a first-hand Qualtrics source in place of a help page. AC3 dropped its one-item module, because no HiTOP-SR scale has fewer than 3 items.
