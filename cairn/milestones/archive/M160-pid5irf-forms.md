# M160: PID-5 informant form (IRF) Word, Qualtrics and REDCap forms

**Status:** done (2026-10-04, PR #169 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop/pull/169)

**Goal:** Researchers can build and download PID-5 IRF paper forms, a Qualtrics import file and a REDCap data dictionary.

**Outcome:** New exports `generate_docx_pid5irf()` (US and A4), `generate_qualtrics_pid5irf()` and `generate_redcap_pid5irf()` share the helper `pid_irf_form()`. It gives the 218 IRF rows with the `TextIRF` wording, the form's opening text, and a page header made of the rating prompt and the "He or she…" stem. Word prints the opening paragraph and puts the prompt and stem in a repeated header row of the item table (`make_items_table(header_note =)`). Qualtrics and REDCap restate them after each page break through a new `page_header` argument of the shared builders. The scoring page lists the 25 facets with IRF numbers and 14 (R) marks. The Word footer prints the form's APA notice, a new `notice` element of `pid_irf_instructions` checked against the PDF, through `build_docx_footer(notice =)`. The other forms keep the Society line, and the other exports did not change. IDs are `PID5IRF_001` to `_218` and fields `pid5irf_001` to `_218`. Four prebuilt files ship with 4 manifest rows (52 in all) and a new download page without an online strip, and there are now 8 pages.

**Decisions:** Milestone-local, with IP1 sign-off at the merge gate: the forms print the form's own text from `pid_irf_instructions` as above. They leave out the name, informant and relationship fields and the "Clinician Use" column, as the other PID-5 forms do. SOURCES.md has a row for the form text.

**Review:** A claim audit read 96 claims and corrected 6. A three-lens fan-out gave 24 findings: 13 fixed, 5 follow-ups and 6 rejected (2 planned, 3 false, 1 style). The largest fix restates the prompt and stem on every page, because later online pages showed items with no subject. Other fixes covered the import-instructions and overview pages, footer tests on both paper sizes and the HiTOP forms, a notice typed from the PDF, the Decisions entry and SOURCES row, and stale counts. The follow-ups went to the "PID-5 norms not yet shipped" row (informant gaps): informant identity fields, APA's no-modification term for all APA PID-5 forms, the IRF rows' "1.0" version and a stale test comment. The `\value` gap stays in the "Clinical reporting & release" row. CI needed two waits.
