# M143: HiTOP-DAT item, answer and scale tables

**Status:** done (2026-10-01, PR #160 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop/pull/160)

**Goal:** The package ships the 382 HiTOP-DAT items, their answer options, their scales and their instructions as documented datasets, with keys checked against published sources.

**Outcome:** `data-raw/hitopdat_info.R` builds `hitopdat_items` (382 rows, battery numbers 1 to 382 in flow order, `MeasureItem` per measure), `hitopdat_choices` (13 answer sets, 58 rows, no "Skip") and `hitopdat_scales` (57 scales, per-scale `reverseNumbers`) from the uncommitted Qualtrics file, or from the committed CSVs without it. `data-raw/sysdata.R` adds `hitopdat_instructions` (six texts, `NA` for CAPE). At Jeff's direction (2026-10-01) the tables are not announced: their help pages carry `@keywords internal`, and NEWS and the `_pkgdown.yml` reference index leave them out. `test-keying-hitopdat.R` checks the CAT-PD facets and 216 texts against the IPIP CAT-PD-SF v1.1 key, the 19 IDAS-II scales and 99 texts against Watson's (2011) key, the 18 Table 1 counts against Watson et al. (2012), the five totals, and the 57 manual names both ways. `test-data-hitopdat.R` checks shapes, text, answer values, instructions and the internal keyword. SOURCES.md "HiTOP-DAT" holds five sources, a category table, four departures (item 194 completed from IPIP, signed off), DAT-1 to DAT-14 and working facts W1 to W4 that need confirmation.

**Decisions:** D-082 (an IDAS-II key its authors send is admissible, with Jeff's sign-off; the 2011 key was signed off 2026-10-01) and D-085 (scale names follow the DAT manual) are in DECISIONS.md. Milestone-local: none.
