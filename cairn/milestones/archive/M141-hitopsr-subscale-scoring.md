# M141: HiTOP-SR scoring can include the 17 subscales on request

**Status:** done (2026-09-29, PR #152 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop/pull/152, merged 2026-09-29T18:29:03Z)

**Goal:** A researcher can get HiTOP-SR subscale scores and subscale reliabilities by setting one argument.

**Outcome:** `score_hitopsr()` and `reliability_hitopsr()` take `include_subscales = FALSE`. With `TRUE`, the rows of `hitopsr_subscales` follow the scales as extra columns or extra reliability rows. Under `calc_se`, subscale `_se` columns follow the scale `_se` columns. A subscale row's `Scale` is its `Subscale` name. Under a `module`, only the subscales whose parent scale the module holds are added. `hitopsr_engine_inputs()` calls the new `add_hitopsr_subscales()` in `R/module.R`. It aborts as an internal error on an unmatched parent name or on a subscale item outside the module. `label_hitopsr(target = "scales")` labels subscale columns. The `interval_hitopsr()` help now says that it converts them. The tests use a hardcoded 17-subscale key, `subscale_key` in `helper-fixtures.R`, taken from the `HiTOP-SR-Final.xlsx` sheet. They also use a hand-computed fixture and `holed_subscales()`. NEWS lists two breaks under Breaking changes: `i =` no longer abbreviates `items`, and a positional `subset` fails. Tests pin both breaks. No keying content changed.

**Decisions:** M141-D1: `include_subscales` sits after `layout` and before the deprecated `subset`. The plan gate chose the name `include_subscales` to match `generate_docx_hitopsr()`. It chose to score in-module subscales over refusing a module. It also chose subscale `_se` columns and no `type` column.

**Review:** two passes, three-lens fan-out each. Pass 1 was defect return 1, because AC1's `_se` placement was asserted under one `missing` mode only. T5 fixed it, and T6 fixed 8 more findings. Pass 2 found 13 findings and no criterion failing. Five were fixed at the gate: a NEWS Breaking changes entry, a positional-`subset` test, `1` in both flag tests, a type-exact `nItems` check, and the `rank_scales()` tie-order clause. A new candidate row covers a hand-edited module, which scores a short scale silently or gives a false internal error. The Word-generator row now names the modules article too. Six findings were rejected as plan choices or not required by a criterion. The PR had no review comments. CI 8 of 8 green. No lesson added (LESSONS at its byte budget).
