# M148: Online form: text for participants, progress, and a way on from errors

**Status:** done (2026-10-01, PR #159 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop/pull/159; companion hitop-form PR #24 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop-form/pull/24)

**Goal:** A participant who opens a study link for the first time reads text written for them and always knows where they are in the form. Every error or saved-file screen gives them a next step.

**Outcome:** In hitop-form `form.js` and `index.html`, `showError()` picks one sentence by the refusal's `kind`. `connection` (an export fetch with no response) says to check the connection and reload. `browser` (a `z` link that `canInflate()` finds this browser cannot unpack, with no `DecompressionStream` or none for `deflate-raw`) says to open another browser. Any other refusal says to contact the study team. The refusal sits in a closed "Details for the study team" section. That section ends every screen after the exports load, with the version lines in a `<footer>`. No shown text names a host or an HTTP status, and completion links read "Continue to the next step of the study". The start screen says an unconfirmed send saves a file. The identifier field has a hint, `aria-describedby`, phone-safe attributes and Enter, which reads `isComposing` only. Item pages show "Page p of n", and "Part k of m" under a list, above the items and beside the button. They also have a closed "Instructions" section, 44 px option labels and missed-item marks. The `p.missed-count` alert is drawn empty with the page. A press writes it two frames after moving it, and an answer rewrites it only for a new number. "I do not agree" asks once. A send fills an empty `p.sending` status line, and a failed send is headed "Your answers were not sent". The new `tests/screens.spec.js` holds P1 to P6, and the suite grew from 846 to 879 tests. hitop NEWS and `online-collection.Rmd` describe the screens.

**Decisions:** D1: every screen after the exports load ends with one closed "Details for the study team" section, the refusal or the send's fault first. D2: "I do not agree" keeps the consent text and swaps the buttons for "Yes, I do not agree" and "Go back". D3: a completion link reads "Continue to the next step of the study".

**Review:** Three passes, each a three-lens fan-out. Pass 1 made one defect return. On Chrome and Edge 80 to 102, a `z` link without `deflate-raw` got the contact sentence (R1). T8 to T10 fixed R1 to R8. Pass 2 made one amendment return. A `c` link on Chrome 80 to 92 fails on `Object.hasOwn` (S1), so "a browser that cannot read the link" had no edge. Two fresh audits followed, and Jeff chose wording that keys each sentence to the shown refusal. T11 to T13 fixed S2 to S10. Pass 3 found no criterion failing. U1 to U15 were fixed at the gate. A reviewer's accessibility-tree probe showed a one-frame write still reached the tree with its text, so the write moved to two frames. The keyCode 229 Enter guard was reverted for Android keyboards. Deferred findings went to the new "Online form participant-screen gaps" row. Jeff routed two limits to Known issue 15. Browsers too old to run the form stay on "Loading". The builder shows a raw error for a `z` link on Chrome and Edge 80 to 102 (S11). R15 to R19, S14 and S15 were rejected. The three claim audits corrected 6, 10 and 3 claims. CI passed on hitop-form #24 (`tests`) and on all 8 checks of hitop #159. One lesson was added (frame timing), and the M034 lesson on the norms mutation scripts was pruned for the byte cap.
