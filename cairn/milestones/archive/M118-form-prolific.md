# M118: A study link can ask hitop-form to take the participant's Prolific ID and send them to a completion URL

**Status:** done (2026-09-24, PR #129 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop/pull/129; hitop-form PR #6 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop-form/pull/6, merged by Jeff from his terminal)

**Goal:** Two link fields make hitop-form fit a Prolific study: `prolific: true` takes `PROLIFIC_PID` from the page's address as the participant identifier and writes `STUDY_ID` and `SESSION_ID` into the row and the file, and `complete` names an `https://` address the participant is sent to after a confirmed send and offered after a saved file.

**Outcome:** In hitop-form: `parseLink()` reads `prolific` (the two booleans only, refused beside a `participant`) and `complete` through `checkCompleteUrl()`, which shares `parseAddress()` with `checkStoreUrl()`, takes `https:` alone, and refuses credentials after the scheme. `readProlific()` reads the three parameters, a `{{%…%}}` placeholder as absent. `leadColumns()` and `leadValues()` give `buildCsv()`, `buildRow()` and `storeSql(table, items, shuffle, prolific)` one lead order: `prolific_study` and `prolific_session` after `submitted` and after `item_order`, always written under `prolific: true`, empty when the address gave nothing. `runForm()` takes the identifier from a non-empty `PROLIFIC_PID` and shows no field. `finish()` calls `location.assign(complete)` on a confirmed send with no sent screen; `showSaved()` adds a "Then continue to <host>." link after the file name. `link.html` has the "Recruit through Prolific" box, the "Completion URL" field, the builder-side refusals, the printed placeholder suffix and the SQL columns. Fixtures: two by-rule SQL files, a by-rule CSV, a shuffled prolific capture. Tests G10, G11, S11 to S17, T13 to T16, L10 to L13, N7. In hitop: the online-collection article's "The Prolific route" and "Who holds the data", the two columns in the modules article, the PID-5 vignette and `?read_form_responses`, `responses-hitopbr-prolific.csv` with its provenance row and reader test, NEWS.
