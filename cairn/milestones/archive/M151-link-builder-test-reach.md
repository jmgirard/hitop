# M151: Study Link Builder test reach

**Status:** done (2026-10-01, PR #158 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop/pull/158; companion hitop-form PR #23 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop-form/pull/23)

**Goal:** The Study Link Builder's tests state their expectations apart from the page's code and reach the states the reviews found untested.

**Outcome:** In hitop-form `tests/`, `link-sections.spec.js` compares `#next` with `NEXT`, a literal table of the 15 site and destination sentences, and its sentence count takes `;`. `readmeSlugs()` skips fenced code, also indented fences, and closes a fence only on the same character at the same or greater length. The spec answers export requests from copies under `tests/fixtures/exports/` (hitop `318629fe`). Its `afterEach` fails on a request to another address that no route answered. The result region is checked with a short `c` link and a `z` link over 5,000 characters at 375px and 1280px. `ONE_FIELD` has a CloudResearch Connect entry, and forced presses drive the four Move guards. L36 in `link.spec.js` fires six `z` refusals (`BAD_Z`) and compares `builderValues()` with a load with no link. `openSectionOf(page, control)` in `helpers.mjs` replaces `openBuilderSections()`. It opens a field's section by a click on its summary, in the nine specs that used the old helper. The suite grew from 831 to 846 tests.

**Decisions:** D1: the export copies are committed files, not fetched per run. The weekly run against the deployed page therefore does not check a new export in `link-sections.spec.js`, and the README and the `fetchExport` comment say so.

**Review:** Two passes, each a three-lens fan-out. Pass 1 returned the milestone once: `link-consent.spec.js` wrote the Consent boxes by `evaluate()` without opening their section (AC6). T8 opened it in `setBox()`, and a plant failed 13 of 17 tests. Pass 2 found AC1-AC7 passing and 7 new findings. Of 29 findings, 4 were fixed at the gate. G1: a `z` prefill opens its section after an async unpack. A click-to-open helper that runs before the prefill's last write closes the section. The test now waits on that write, and the comment there records the lesson. The gate also fixed a comment (G3), two texts on the copies (F4) and the fence rule (F8). Seven went to the Study Link Builder candidate row, now "edge cases and test gaps (M149, M151 reviews)". N7 in `network.spec.js` fails about 1 run in 10, also on `main`. 18 were rejected with reasons. PR #158 had no review comments. CI passed: hitop-form #23 with 846 of 846, and hitop #158 with all 8 checks.
