# M073: Every exported function the package still recommends is demonstrated or linked in the vignettes

**Status:** done (2026-08-30, PR #79 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop/pull/79)

**Goal:** Close the six gaps between what `NAMESPACE` exports and what the shipped vignettes and articles demonstrate, and ship the sweep that found them as a test so the gap does not reopen on the next export.

**Outcome:** No package behavior changed. `tests/testthat/test-vignette-export-coverage.R` classifies each of the 39 `export()` entries against the 15 `.Rmd` files under `vignettes/` (5 top-level, 10 under `articles/`) as called in an evaluated `{r}` chunk, linked as `reference/<name>.html`, or exempt because its body calls a `deprecate_*()` helper; `split_rmd()` drops chunks disabled by header (`eval = FALSE`, `eval=F`) or body option (`#| eval: false`) and strips comments, `called_in()` requires `name(` rather than a mention, and eight fixture arms hold the classifier to that — including the two positives that must stay silent. Floors of 39 exports and 15 files with assertions that the glob reaches both directories keep either enumeration from silently emptying. Five exports were uncovered and now are not: `vignettes/pid5_scoring.Rmd` gained `## Rank Each Person's Highest Facets`, ranking the 25 facet columns with `rank_scales(..., top = 5, append = FALSE)`; `hitopsr_scoring.Rmd` and `hitopbr_scoring.Rmd` each gained `## Labelling Columns`, showing `label_hitopsr()` / `label_hitopbr()` attaching item text to a raw item column and the printed scale name to a scored one; and `vignettes/articles/download-hitophsum.Rmd` gained a Custom File Generation card linking `generate_docx_hitophsum()` and `generate_redcap_hitophsum()`, the only two generators that instrument exports.

**Decisions:** None cross-cutting; no `DECISIONS.md` entry. Four plan-gate choices with recorded falsifiers, the load-bearing ones being to drop the standing ask for `calc_se` worked examples (the argument warns and is scheduled for removal, so a demonstration would teach its use) and to ship the sweep as a permanent test rather than a one-time check.

**Review:** Three fresh-context reviewers on distinct evidence bases ([O] diff-bug, [S] blame-history, [S] prior-review-record) returned fourteen findings, all from the diff lens; none met the return floor. Six fixed on the branch: the `rank_scales()` chunk printed a 100 x 31 tibble in which `top_scales` fell into the truncation, so the section never showed its own result; the HiTOP-HSUM lead claimed the package builds all three downloads when it exports no Qualtrics generator; `eval=F` escaped the unevaluated-chunk test (a fixture arm added and shown red first); a dead `is_text` vector carried a comment stating an invariant nothing enforced; the non-emptiness floors were `>= 30`/`>= 10` against actual counts of 39 and 15; and `get0()` walked the namespace's parents. Six filed as one candidate row (sweep reach: the deprecation exemption cannot tell a deprecated function from a deprecated argument, the sweep skips under `R CMD check` so CI never runs it, string-literal and fence parsing, unchecked link targets, positional facet selection). Two rejected. `devtools::test()` FAIL 0 / WARN 0, `devtools::check()` 0/0/0, `check_pkgdown()` clean, `cairn_validate` exit 0, all eight CI checks green. Nothing graduated or retired at hygiene.
