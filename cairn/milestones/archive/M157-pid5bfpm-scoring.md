# M157: PID5BF+M keying and scoring

**Status:** done (2026-10-03, PR #166 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop/pull/166)

**Goal:** Researchers can score the 36-item PID5BF+M (Bach et al., 2020) with `score_pid5()` and `reliability_pid5()`.

**Outcome:** `score_pid5()`, `reliability_pid5()`, `rename_pid5_items()` and `label_pid5()` take `version = "BFPM"`. Item columns are `pid5bfpm_01` to `pid5bfpm_36`. `data-raw/pid_bfpm_key.csv` holds the FU Berlin key sheet's table (p. 2). `data-raw/pid_info.R` builds `pid_items$BFPM`, `pid_scales$BFPM` (18 two-item facets in key order) and the new exported `pid_bfpm_domains` (6 domains, Anankastia fifth) from it. Scores are item means, and each domain is the mean of its 3 facets through `score_engine()`'s existing `domain_map` path. `reliability_pid5()` adds the 6 domain rows at call time. The reliability engine skips omega for a scale under 3 items. `?score_pid5`, `?reliability_*`, the scoring vignette and NEWS state the factor of 2 against the key sheet's sums. They also state the `"apa"` rule, equal to `"complete"` for whole-number responses, and that no total score or reverse-keyed item exists. `version = "B"` no longer abbreviates `"BF"` (NEWS, Breaking changes). `data-raw/characterize_bfpm.R` showed 80 of 80 FULL, SF and BF calls identical to the merge base. Oracles O-008 to O-010 are in `cairn/ORACLES.md`. References: `fuberlin2020pid5bfpm`, `bach2020`.

**Decisions:** D-088 (from RB06/RR06): the names, the item-mean metric, `pid_bfpm_domains`, the `missing` default, omega `NA` under 3 items, and no total. Milestone-local: the omega guard sits in the reliability engine, not in the exported `calc_omega()`. Jeff signed off the keying and waived a deprecation cycle for `"B"` at the merge gate.
