# M146: Study Link Builder: required choices first, optional ones folded away

**Status:** done (2026-09-30, PR #154 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop/pull/154; companion hitop-form PR #19 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop-form/pull/19)

**Goal:** A researcher who opens the Study Link Builder for the first time sees the required choices first. The optional features sit in named sections, the hints are short, and the finished link comes with its next step.

**Outcome:** In hitop-form `link.html`, the required parts (instruments, study name, where responses go) come first. Five closed `<details>` sections follow ("Participants and recruiting site", "Item order and HiTOP-SR module", "Consent", "When the participant finishes", "Your own questions"), each with a summary of "Not used" or the labels of its filled fields. A prefilled field opens its section, and a refusal opens the section and focuses the field. Move up, Move down and Remove are disabled where they do nothing, and their names begin with their visible text. A "Your study link" region holds a scrolling box, "Copy the link" and one next-step sentence from `nextStep()`, and takes focus. A field change hides it, and a build that a field change interrupts shows a message instead. Hints are 40 words or fewer and the intro 60 or fewer, with detail in new README sections. `link.html` and the `form.js` messages it shows use D-083's names. `tests/instruments-store.spec.js` became `tests/instruments-supabase.spec.js`, and the new `tests/link-sections.spec.js` (S1 to S9) covers the criteria. In hitop, the Instruments menu reads "Module Builder" and "Study Link Builder", the two online-collection articles and `README.Rmd` take the names, and NEWS has the entry.

**Decisions:** none milestone-local. The names come from D-083.

**Review:** Three passes, three-lens fan-out each time, with two defect returns. Pass 1 found no NEWS entry and a retired term in a spec file name (AC6). Pass 2 found a two-sentence next step for Supabase with no site (AC4). Pass 3 passed AC1 to AC7. Its gate fixed three findings: the SQL and instruments hints name the table's columns and when to make a new table, the destination hint tells a JSON row from table columns, and an interrupted build says so. The link-gaps candidate row was promoted at hygiene to a `[high]` plan-next row with the pass-3 follow-ups. The Chromium-only decoding tests went to DESIGN Known issue 13. Two participant-page notes went to M148's work log, and D-074's old menu labels to the "User-facing names" row.
