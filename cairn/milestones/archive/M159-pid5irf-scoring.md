# M159: PID-5 informant form (IRF) keying and scoring

**Status:** done (2026-10-04, PR #168 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop/pull/168)

**Goal:** Researchers can score the 218-item PID-5 Informant Form (Markon et al., 2013) with `score_pid5()` and `reliability_pid5()`.

**Outcome:** `score_pid5()`, `reliability_pid5()`, `rename_pid5_items()` and `label_pid5()` take `version = "IRF"`. Output is the full form's 25 facets and 5 domains under the same names, with domains from `pid_domains`. `pid_items` gains `IRF` and `TextIRF`. `IRF` is `NA` on self-report items 96 and 177, so IRF n is self-report n, n + 1 or n + 2. `pid_scales` gains `IRF`, and internal `pid_irf_instructions` holds start, continue, prompt, stem and options. `data-raw/pid_irf_items.csv` is built from the shelf APA PDF. `data-raw/check_pid_irf_text.R` reads the PDF in raw mode and checks all 218 texts, the 25 facets, the mapping and the instructions. Items are named `pid5irf_001` to `pid5irf_218`. Tests: keying typed from the key (tables in `helper-fixtures.R`), 150 hand-computed values on `fx_pid5irf()`, an independent recomputation on random data under all three missing modes, and full label and rename sweeps. `characterize_bfpm.R` gained a BFPM pairing; 100 of 100 calls were identical at the merge base. References page `apa2013pid5irf`, SOURCES.md OQ-4 and OQ-5, help pages, vignette section, NEWS and README.

**Decisions:** D-089: version `"IRF"`, two `pid_items` columns, the Facet Table's 14 reverse items (Step 1's 98 and 176 read as a printing error), and `pid_domains`. D-090 (from RB07/RR07, a Fable review): proration keeps the nearest-whole-number rule, halves up, not the key's printed "round up". Jeff signed off both at the T2 gate and the merge gate.
