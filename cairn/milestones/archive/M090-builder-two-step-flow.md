# M090: A two-step builder flow with the format settings folded away

**Status:** done (2026-09-11, PR #98 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop/pull/98 tracking, code in jmgirard/hitop-builder PR #15 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop-builder/pull/15)

**Goal:** The HiTOP-SR Module Builder asks for scales, then for a format, and downloads from that second screen, with every format setting behind a closed disclosure that shows its current values.

**Outcome:** In hitop-builder, the three `section.step` blocks became two (*Choose scales*, *Choose a format and download*) and `STEP_IDS` has two entries. A `[data-choose]` card press calls `setFormat()` only: it hides the other `.fmtpanel`s, closes every `details.settings`, marks the card `aria-current` through `markFormatChoice()`, relabels `#downloadBtn` from `FORMATS[].button`, and writes "Ready. <label> chosen." to `#status` while the page is idle. The *Choose a different format* button, `FORMATS[].heading` and `#step3h` are gone. Each format's fieldsets sit in one `<details class="settings">` whose `<summary>` is written by `refreshSummaries()`/`settingsSummary()` from the controls on `input`/`change`. The disclosure is closed at first paint and after every card press. `#shuffleNote` sits below the Word disclosure, outside it, shown while the box is ticked. `tilesExactly` became `itemsRunFromOne`; its log line and the two README passages say the scales' items run from 1 with no gaps and nothing more. README's *What the page shows* walks two steps and names the settings line, the warning and the status line. `tests/smoke.spec.js` keeps the step-bar-then-Word-card route with its comment rewritten. This repo: tracking only.
