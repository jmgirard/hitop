# M161: PID-5 child forms (ages 11 to 17)

**Status:** done (2026-10-04, PR #170 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop/pull/170)

**Goal:** Researchers can build, download and score the APA PID-5 child forms for ages 11 to 17 (220-item full form, 25-item brief form).

**Outcome:** `data-raw/check_pid_child_text.R` compares the two APA child PDFs with the package tables. It finds the 245 item texts in order, the reverse list, the Facet Table, the row-bound Domain Table and the BF key equal to `pid_items`, `pid_scales$FULL`, `pid_scales$BF` and `pid_domains`. It also checks the stored instructions, notices and labels (page `cairn/references/apa2013pid5child.md`). So `score_pid5()` scores the child forms as `FULL` and `BF`, and no keying table changed. New exports `generate_{docx,qualtrics,redcap}_pid5child()` and `generate_{docx,qualtrics,redcap}_pid5bfchild()` share `pid_child_form()` and `build_docx_pid5_child()`. They print the adult items with the child instructions (`pid_child_instructions` in `R/sysdata.rda`) and the adult scoring page, and the Word footer carries the APA notice. Online exports use the adult names (`PID5_001`/`pid5_001`, `PID5BF_01`/`pid5bf_01`). Eight files ship with 8 manifest rows (60 in all) and two download pages without an online strip (10 pages). No adult artifact or generator signature changed. The help, scoring vignette, NEWS, README, import-instructions page, DESIGN and a SOURCES row were updated.

**Decisions:** M161-D1 (Jeff, T2 gate: new functions, adult item names) was promoted as D-091, which annotates D-055.

The AC1 re-audit also tightened the script: it now reads the dataset and binds each domain to its row (the old check passed a swapped pair) and checks the BF reverse flags. They also added non-default-argument tests, a docs sweep, D-091 and the SOURCES row. The follow-ups went to a new row, "PID-5 child form gaps": no APA notice online, omitted page text and identity fields, the shared test lists and stale counts.
