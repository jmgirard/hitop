# M133: A study link can take the participant's identifier from a named address parameter and put it into the completion address, so hitop-form fits SONA and CloudResearch Connect

**Status:** done (2026-09-28, PR #144 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop/pull/144, merged 2026-09-28T14:07:13Z, and companion hitop-form PR #13 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop-form/pull/13, merged 2026-09-28T04:27:11Z)

**Goal:** A study link's `participantParam` field names the address parameter holding the participant's identifier, and a `{participant}` token in `complete` or `completeSaved` is replaced by that identifier, so a study recruited through SONA or CloudResearch Connect runs on hitop-form as a Prolific study does.

**Outcome:** hitop-form form.js has `checkParticipantParam()` (1 to 64 of `A-Z a-z 0-9 _ . -`, not `c` or a Prolific name, refused beside a participant or `prolific: true`) and `readParticipantParam()` (the first value not blank, `%…%` or `{{…}}`, else the start screen asks). It also has `fillParticipant()`, used by the navigation, the sent screen's link and `showSaved()`. `checkCompleteUrl()` refuses the token in the host or path, and refuses spellings the page would not fill: another letter case, doubled braces, a space inside the braces, `%7B`/`%7D`/`%257B`/`%257D`. No column is added. link.html has a "Recruiting site" menu (None, Prolific, SONA with `id` and `&id=%SURVEY_CODE%`, Connect with `participantId`, Another site with a typed name) in place of the Prolific box, with prefill by site. The chosen hint is tied to the menu by `aria-describedby`. Tests: guard.spec.js G14 to G16, link.spec.js L24 to L31, and recruit.spec.js P1 to P14 (343 passed). hitop has the article's "The SONA and CloudResearch Connect routes" section, the `read_form_responses()` help, NEWS, and source notes `sona2026help.md` and `cloudresearch2026help.md`.
