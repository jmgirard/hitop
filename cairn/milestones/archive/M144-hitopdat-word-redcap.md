# M144: HiTOP-DAT Word forms and REDCap dictionary

**Status:** done (2026-10-16, PR #180 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop/pull/180)

**Goal:** Researchers can build and download HiTOP-DAT paper forms and a REDCap data dictionary made from the M143 tables.

**Outcome:** `generate_docx_hitopdat()` writes US and A4 Word forms: the 382 items in battery order, one section per measure with its instruction (none for the CAPE; "\n" printed as line breaks by `instruction_fpar()`), and one items table per run of items sharing an answer set, each with its own legend and option columns. `build_hitop_doc()` gained `sections`; `legend_per_line()` keeps a legend on one line up to 80 characters at 10 points on a 7-inch width, scaled by font and page. No scoring page. The footer (`hitopdat_instructions$notice`, `data-raw/sysdata.R`) names the seven measures with the author and year of their headings in the revised manual's "Measures and reference data", the WHODAS credited to WHO without the printed 1988 (SOURCES.md DAT-16). `generate_redcap_hitopdat()` writes `hdat_001` to `hdat_382` with per-item choices and six `hdat_<measure>_instructions` fields; each measure after the first starts a page, and in-measure breaks restate the instruction. `build_redcap_zip()` gained `choices` and `sections`, with `insert_page_breaks()` and `redcap_descriptive_row()` factored out; other instruments' output is unchanged, locked against their shipped files. New `validate_positive_number()`. Three manifest rows, site copies, `download-hitopdat.Rmd` (11 pages; card and Qualtrics checks read the manifest), navbar, NEWS, reference index, README row, DESIGN.md. SOURCES.md records the redistribution permission and the seven citations.
