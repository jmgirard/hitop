# M135: A study link can carry the researcher's consent text, which hitop-form shows before the form with an agree and a decline choice

**Status:** done (2026-09-28, PR #146 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop/pull/146, merged 2026-09-28T17:49:03Z, and companion hitop-form PR #15 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop-form/pull/15, merged 2026-09-28T17:24:15Z)

**Goal:** A study link's `consent` field holds the researcher's consent text. The page shows it on a screen of its own before the start screen, and "I do not agree" ends the session with nothing sent or saved.

**Outcome:** hitop-form form.js has `checkConsent()` in `parseLink()`. It takes `text` up to 20,000 characters and an optional `declined` up to 2,000, and refuses blank, type, key and lone-surrogate faults. `completeDeclined` goes through `checkCompleteUrl()` and is accepted only beside `consent`. `decodeLink()` reads `c` or `z`. `inflateConfig()` reads `z` through `DecompressionStream("deflate-raw")` with a 100,000-byte cap, then decodes it with `TextDecoder` `fatal: true`. `encodeLink()` writes `z`, and `checkParticipantParam()` refuses `z`. `runForm()` shows the consent screen ("Consent to take part") and the declined screen ("Thank you"), built with `el()` as text. The declined screen is drawn before the move to `completeDeclined`. link.html has Consent text and Declined text boxes, a decline address, their checks, `z` output and `z` prefill. New specs: consent (C1 to C7), link-consent (K1 to K7), zlink (Z1 to Z5), 453 passed. Rows, file and Supabase SQL keep their columns. hitop: the online-collection article section "Consent text before the form", and a NEWS entry.

**Decisions:** D-079 (a), (b) and (d), recorded at the plan gate. The implement gate chose the two headings and placed the three builder fields after the completion URLs.
