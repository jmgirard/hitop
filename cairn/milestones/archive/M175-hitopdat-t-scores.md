# M175: HiTOP-DAT T scores and the suicide-risk alert

**Status:** done (2026-11-26, PR #183 https://github.com/jmgirard/hitop/pull/183)

**Goal:** `norm_hitopdat()` turns `score_hitopdat()` scores into T scores against the norms the authors name, and marks Suicidality and Self-Harm T scores at or above an alert point.

**Outcome:** `norm_hitopdat(data, prefix = "dat_", alert_t = 65, append = TRUE)` in `R/norm_hitopdat.R` returns 53 `_t` columns, `round_half_up(50 + 10 * (score - M) / SD)`, then `_suicidality_alert` and `_selfHarm_alert` on the rounded T. New dataset `hitopdat_norms` (Measure, Scale, camelCase, M, SD, Sample, N, Source), built by `data-raw/hitopdat_norms.R` from `data-raw/hitopdat_norms.csv`: WHODAS from Andrews Table 1 total, 18 IDAS-II scales from Watson Table 2 "Adults" (sums), CAPE from Galletly Table 1 (n-weighted mean and the common SD, per item; computed in the script; N NA), 33 CAT-PD facets from Simms's workbook E3:F35 (N NA). A NaN score gives NA. Refusals: missing or duplicated score columns (named in full), non-numeric and `integer64` columns (`hitop_nonnumeric_items`, via a new `class` argument of `validate_numeric_columns()`), `alert_t`, `prefix`, `append`, output collisions. AUDIT, DUDIT and PHQ-15 get no T score. Source notes galletly2016 and simms2026catpdworkbook; sample text added to andrews2009 and watson2012; SOURCES.md DAT-15 (b) marked answered. Typed norms in `tests/testthat/helper-hitopdat-norms.R`; append-collision and error-prose sweeps gained probes; the download page links the function.

**Decisions:** D-101 (`hitop_nonnumeric_items` covers this score-column refusal, annotating D-068). AC5 amended after review: the PHQ-15 gets no T score because the authors keep it on its cutoffs (Jonas, 2026-11-18, read as the joint answer).
